Why "Fencing Token"?

In distributed systems, a fencing token is a number that increases every time a lock is granted. Storage rejects any write carrying an older token, so a process whose authority has gone stale can never act on it. That is the principle here: authority is checked where the action happens, by a system that can say no. AI agents are the first series; the idea applies to any system that acts. (Not a cryptocurrency.)
